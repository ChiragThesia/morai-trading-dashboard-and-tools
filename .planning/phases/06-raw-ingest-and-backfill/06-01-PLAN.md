<objective>

existing client and landed in two tables — the broker's own raw copy and the fills derived from it —
proven end to end through a real Procrastinate worker run.

Purpose: this phase's real risk is not the extraction arithmetic. It is the seam nobody has crossed
yet: the worker process holds a psycopg pool for Procrastinate and no SQLAlchemy session at all, so
until a job actually opens an RLS-evaluated `morai_app` session, writes two tables through two write
paths in one transaction, and commits, the execution model `D6-01` picked is a claim rather than a
fact. Proving the whole path on one transaction first means an architectural dead end costs one
commit instead of six.

`morai.ingest` package holding the pure vendor-payload extraction and the `sync_user` shell; the

bind-parameter-ceiling proofs.
