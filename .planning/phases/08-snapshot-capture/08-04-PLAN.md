<objective>
`D8-15`, made real: a `snapshot_runs` row per capture attempt, mirroring Phase 6's `sync_runs`, plus
the query that turns Procrastinate's own scheduler hole into something a person can see.

Purpose: `L042`. In v1 the GEX open interest read zero for an extended period with the vendor endpoint
confirmed live — the adapter's own scheduled job had silently stopped and never resumed, and nothing
existed to tell a stalled job apart from a real outage. `L043` is the same failure one level down: a
job that logs nothing on success makes "healed nothing", "never ran" and "errored per-slot"
indistinguishable. A gap-density heuristic cannot resolve either. A row proving the job ran at all is
the only thing that does.

This plan also closes the hole research finding 2 named: Procrastinate's `PeriodicDeferrer` backfills
only the most recent missed tick and only under ten minutes old, so a longer worker outage produces no
job, and therefore no gap row anywhere. That slot is invisible in `snapshot_observations` and
`snapshot_marks` by construction. `missing_capture_slots` is what makes it visible, and plan 08-03's
backfill is what makes it repairable.

Output: `src/morai/ingest/snapshot_runs.py`, the two-session run accounting in `snapshot_user_task`,
and the missing-slot query.
</objective>
