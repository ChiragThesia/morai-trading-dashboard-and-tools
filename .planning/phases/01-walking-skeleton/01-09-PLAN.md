`NUMERIC(14,4)` permits at most 14 significant digits and an IEEE-754 double carries about 15.95,
so no value both fits the column and visibly loses a digit. The criterion was satisfied in
substance by asserting bit-inexactness — `Decimal(float(x)) != Decimal(x)` — for
`Decimal("9999999999.9999")` and `Decimal("1234567890.1234")`. Note that `Decimal("7425.5000")`
was deliberately not used: it is an exact binary fraction and would have passed for the wrong
reason. **Do not edit the ROADMAP's wording.** Record the discrepancy where verification reads it.

Add a short section pointing at `V092` for criterion 1's measurement rather than restating it, and
listing which of the six criteria each plan closed.
