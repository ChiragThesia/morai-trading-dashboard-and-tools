and present it as contemporaneous. A gap recorded honestly is worth more than a fabricated pair, and
fabricating one would violate the law this phase exists to enforce.

**Criterion 4 wording note.** The criterion asks for a value carrying more precision than a float can
hold. `NUMERIC(14,4)` permits at most 14 significant digits; an IEEE-754 double carries about 15.95;
so no value both fits the column and visibly loses a digit. Satisfied in substance by asserting
bit-inexactness — `Decimal(float(x)) != Decimal(x)` — for `Decimal("9999999999.9999")` and
`Decimal("1234567890.1234")`. Note that `Decimal("7425.5000")` was deliberately not used: an exact
binary fraction, it would have passed for the wrong reason. Point at
`tests/test_decimal_canary.py` for the pure-Python proof and at plan 01-08's criterion 4 evidence for
the authoritative Railway proof.

**Criterion 1 wording note.** The criterion asks that the deployed service answer its health check
over both IPv4 and IPv6 from a single `[::]` bind. Railway's public edge is IPv4-only — no AAAA
record on `healthcheck.railway.app` from three independent resolvers — so the IPv6 half cannot be
served over the public edge by any service, regardless of its bind. It was proven instead over
Railway's private network, from the worker service to
`http://<web>.railway.internal:$PORT/health`, which is the path the dual-stack bind actually exists
for. Record that the public `curl -6` failure is the expected-failure control confirming the
IPv4-only edge, not a phase failure. Record the verdict: **V039 re-measured and CONFIRMED**, and the
`[::]` bind stays. Point at `V092`.

**Do not edit the ROADMAP's wording** for either. Record both discrepancies here.

Add a short closing section listing which plan closed which of the six criteria.
