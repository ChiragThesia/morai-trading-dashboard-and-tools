<objective>
Phase goal (ROADMAP, verbatim): "What the user said they would do is captured before the position
opens, and structurally cannot change afterwards."

INTENT-08 names four closed vocabularies: structure, entry trigger, exit reason, plan-followed.
Three of them shipped in plans 10-01 and 10-02 from sourced values. This plan closes the fourth.

Purpose: `entry_trigger`'s member values are the one thing in this phase the project's own record does
not settle. The research searched `docs/learnings/`, `knowledge-base/`, `salvage/` and
`docs/rebuild-research/` and found the field described only as "which rule fired" — no enumeration
anywhere. `D10-08` forbids inventing one, and `D10-18` records the developer's decision to supply the
values directly. So this plan opens with a blocking checkpoint and does nothing until it is answered.

Everything else in the phase was built to be executable without those values, which is why this plan
is last and alone. Its second task is small: one migration adding one column, one enum, one request
field, and the tests that pin the two layers together.

**This plan is not autonomous.** Task 1 carries `gate="blocking-human"` and is never auto-approved,
in any mode. Auto-approving it would mean an agent choosing the vocabulary, which is precisely what
`D10-08` exists to prevent.
</objective>
