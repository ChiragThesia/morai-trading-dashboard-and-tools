<objective>
Prove criterion 5: connection health reads back as healthy, expiring-soon or expired with
an `expires_at`, alongside a last-sync timestamp that is honestly null because no sync
exists yet.

Purpose: the two ways to get this wrong are both quiet. Anchoring the clock to the last
refresh instead of the original grant resets it on every automatic refresh and makes the
seven-day expiry invisible — the user's connection dies with no warning that was ever
displayed. And writing a plausible value into `last_synced_at` before any sync exists turns
a gap into a lie, which is exactly what `NN-16` was written after.

Output: the health function's bands proven at their boundaries with an injected `now`, the
anchor proven stable across a refresh, the two honest-gap columns proven null, and D4-15's
limit written into the code rather than only into a plan.
</objective>
