<objective>
One real calendar's four fills, already in Postgres through the one write path, resolved to
their position, classified from the broker's own `positionEffect`, netted into an OPEN debit
and a CLOSE credit, and written back through `insert_events` — every layer this phase touches,
wired end to end, on one path.

Purpose: the phase's real difficulty is not the arithmetic. It is the split between what runs
in SQL against the plaintext column set, what runs as a pure function over decrypted
`Decimal`s, and what touches the session. Get that split wrong and the oracle becomes
untestable without a database, the fault suite has no seam to inject into, and the scoped-read
bug that cost v1 production comes back. Proving the whole path on one calendar first means an
architectural dead end costs one commit instead of ten.

Output: `src/morai/ledger/pairing.py` holding the promoted disambiguation SQL, the pure
derivation core, and the session shell; one calendar proved end to end; the purity and
no-broker-call gates; and idempotent re-derivation over a `(user, order_id)` scope.
</objective>
