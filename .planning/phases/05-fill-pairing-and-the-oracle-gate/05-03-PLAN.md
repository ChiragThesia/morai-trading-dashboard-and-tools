<objective>
The gate this phase exists for: all 13 real calendars, their 26 independently-computed figures,
the 14th synthetic control, the global invariants, and a fault suite that proves the assertions
have teeth.

Purpose: this suite is an oracle and not a regression snapshot. Every expected number was
computed from the signed cash flows of 13 real Schwab transactions before the fix was written,
independently of the pipeline it tests. That is what makes it worth passing. The fault suite is
what stops it from quietly becoming a snapshot later — a test that cannot fail is not evidence.

Output: `tests/ledger/test_oracle_gate.py` with one parametrized case per calendar and the four
global invariants, and `tests/ledger/test_pairing_seeded_faults.py` proving the three named
fault classes are all caught.
</objective>
