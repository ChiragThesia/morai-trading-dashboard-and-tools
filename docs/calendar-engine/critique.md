## 1. Doctrine gaps — decisions the dossier does not make

The dossier's §8 lists 23 places the *corpus* is silent. That is not the same list as the decisions the *engine* must take. These are the open ones, each with the realistic options.

**1.1 What is the Forward Factor gate, given SPX contango?** The dossier calls this "the single biggest engine decision" (§2.7, §7.5) and answers it with "percentile band primary, absolute 16–20% as a conviction tier." That answer is unbuildable: the band needs a year of daily FF per DTE bucket and the repo has ~27 days of history (current-state §6). Options: (a) rank FF **cross-sectionally within today's snapshot** — percentile across the candidate set, not across time; zero history required; (b) ship the absolute 16–20% gate and accept that "no candidates" is the honest daily output for months; (c) no gate — rank on FF ordinally, start the history, add the band later. The dossier picks the one option the data cannot support.

**1.2 Which of the three term-structure terms actually scores?** `cushion = IV_back − σ_fwd`, `FF = IV_front/σ_fwd − 1`, and `slope = (IV_back − IV_front)/(T_b − T_f)` are all monotone functions of the same two IVs and two DTEs. §7.1 lists all three as encodable score terms. Scoring all three triple-counts one signal. Options: (a) cushion scores, FF and slope display — cushion is the only one that *is* the loss condition (`kre-a-case-study-in-forward-volatility`); (b) FF scores, because it is the author's own named ranking metric; (c) slope scores, because it is the only one that survives an inverted structure without a guard. Pick one; the dossier does not.

**1.3 What is the front leg's forecast RV, in one expression?** §2.3's blend needs 3–4 inputs. One is proprietary (§8.10), two need per-tenor IV history that does not exist, and the published weights are not even self-consistent (§8.9: GLD's 14.1% is not the mean of 10.8/17/14.5). Options: (a) one term, RV20 close-to-close (`realized-vol.ts` already ships it); (b) two-term blend RV20/RV60 with weights you invent; (c) EWMA or GARCH with a decay constant the corpus never gives (§8.6).

**1.4 Does `VRP_front − VRP_back` add anything?** §7.1 proposes it. With a single flat forecast RV for both legs it collapses algebraically to `IV_front − IV_back` — pure slope, collinear with 1.2. To be independent it needs a *tenor-differentiated* forecast, which needs the history from 1.3. Options: (a) drop it, score `IV_front − RV20` on the front leg only (independent, uses realized vol, is the house thesis); (b) keep the difference and accept that it is slope wearing a VRP label; (c) build the per-tenor forecast first.

**1.5 Where is ATM measured, and does the measured strike equal the traded strike?** §2.10 + §4.9 say measure at 50Δ per expiry, trade one strike, then "correct the IV comparison for the skew slope between them" — and §8.13 says the corpus supplies no skew term structure to correct with. Options: (a) measure at 50Δ per expiry, trade the front's 50Δ strike, leave the back IV skew-contaminated; (b) measure both IVs at the one traded strike — skew-consistent, term-structure reading contaminated by differing moneyness; (c) interpolate both to fixed moneyness K/S = 1.00. Note the cost: `ChainQuoteForPicker` (`packages/core/src/picker/application/ports.ts:47-65`) carries **no delta**, so (a) and (c) require solving delta per row from `bsmIv` + carry.

**1.6 Is the ranked unit a pair, or a pair × strike?** The doctrine is entirely pair-level. The incumbent scans front expiry × strike × back expiry (`candidate-selection.ts:392-426`) while the score is nearly strike-invariant. Options: (a) rank (front, back) expiry pairs and derive the strike deterministically; (b) keep the triple loop and let `deltaNeutral`/`debitFit` discriminate strikes; (c) rank pairs, emit top-N pairs × three strikes for the human. The trader's "every candidate calendar" is ambiguous between them.

**1.7 What is edge denominated in, and what is the accept bar?** §3.4 wants edge ÷ margin and the case-study bar of 0.67–3.5%. A dollar edge needs a reprice at forecast vol on **both** legs, and all the doctrine's forecast machinery is 30-day. Options: (a) vol points only, no dollar reprice, no accept bar; (b) reprice both legs at one flat forecast — which the root-time law (§4.3) says produces zero by construction; (c) reprice the front at forecast RV and the back at σ_fwd, i.e. price the calendar as if forward vol were realized. (c) is the only self-consistent version and the corpus never states it.

**1.8 Which fill price ranks?** §2.17 says bid on what you sell, ask on what you buy. The repo's `haircutFill` (`candidate-selection.ts:235-238`) crosses 0.66 of the width — strictly between mid and natural. Options: (a) keep 0.66 (measured, already load-bearing); (b) full natural, which is what the doctrine literally says and kills more candidates; (c) 0.66 for ranking, natural for the accept-bar check.

**1.9 What blocks entry, on what series?** Every block in the doctrine is a percentile (VIX 80th, whole-curve IV pct > 80, IV pct band 65–80 — and §8.21 admits that band is the dossier's own inference). Options: (a) absolute VIX level, the existing `VIX_BLOCK_ARM = 25` (`entry-gate.ts:80`); (b) percentile gated on a minimum sample count; (c) no vol gate at all in v1.

**1.10 Macro dates — positive, negative, or neither?** §8.3 concedes this is the biggest SPX gap. §7.2 then asks for *both* "front-window event → deprioritise" and "back-only event → positive", which together make the FOMC calendar, not vol, the dominant discriminator across ~4 expiry buckets. Options: (a) hard-exclude fronts spanning FOMC/CPI (`EVENT_BLACKOUT_DAYS = 3`); (b) score as a penalty — the repo already tried 0.5 and softened to 0.25 because it saturated (`rules.ts:78-85`); (c) emit as a flag, score nothing, let the human run §7.4's "find out WHY".

**1.11 What does delta-neutral mean for a same-strike calendar?** A same-strike put calendar's net delta is small by construction, so a 15-point `deltaNeutral` term may be near-constant across candidates and discriminate nothing. Options: (a) keep it as a score term (`DELTA_NEUTRAL_MAX = 5`); (b) demote it to the *strike-selection rule* — pick the strike that zeroes net delta — and drop the score term; (c) hard filter at a ±30 position-delta band.

**1.12 Who owns the daily re-entry re-score?** §3.5 makes re-scoring every open calendar with the entry criterion mandatory. Options: (a) one scorer, two callers (candidates and open positions); (b) leave exits with the existing exit advisor; (c) the ranker publishes a threshold and the exit engine compares. The dossier states the requirement and never assigns the module.

**1.13 Reverse calendars.** §8.19 says the sell side is under-specified. "Front ≥ 15 DTE, gap ≥ 15" is direction-agnostic. Options: (a) long only; (b) symmetric with a signed score; (c) long only, but flag FF-below-lower-bound cases as "do not buy" rather than "sell."

**1.14 Sample-size floor for any computed statistic.** Not in the dossier at all. Options: return null below N; report with a visible sample count; report ungated (what the repo does today — current-state landmine 17).

## 3. The minimum engine

**Three new modules, four reused, zero new ports, zero new tables.**

Reused unchanged: `packages/core/src/picker/domain/fwd-iv.ts` (36 lines — `computeFwdIv` is homogeneous in T, so DTE-in-days is fine); `bsmGreeks` from `packages/quant/src/bsm.ts` (also the only source of delta, since the chain read carries none); `haircutFill` (`candidate-selection.ts:235-238`); and the read path `picker-chain.ts` → `ForReadingChainForPicker` → `getChain.ts` (109), the only root-correct, OI-repaired cohort read. `picker/application/ports.ts` already declares everything needed.

New, in `packages/core`:

- **`cohort.ts` (~120 lines)** — group the flat chain by `(root, expiration)`; per cohort resolve one spot, the ATM strike, the ATM IV, and each row's delta from `bsmIv` + that expiry's carry. This is `apps/web/src/lib/chain-math.ts:113-183` plus the delta solve from `chain-risk-reversal.ts`, promoted, not rewritten. Every function takes `root` in its signature so it cannot be called root-blind.
- **`pair.ts` (~120 lines)** — enumerate front cohort × back cohort under the two DTE gates, pick one strike per pair (the front cohort's 50Δ put), price both legs through **one** T function that takes `root`, and return `{fwdIv, guard, cushion, ff, slope, netGreeks, debit, spreadPct}`.
- **`score.ts` (~60 lines)** — the weighted sum, four terms.

**Score terms — four, each a different signal:**

| Term | Formula | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| `fwdCushion` | `IV_back − σ_fwd`, vol points, normalised over a configured range | 45 |
| `frontVrp` | `IV_front − RV20`, vol points, floored at 0 | 25 |
| `deltaNeutral` | `1 − \|netΔ\| / DELTA_NEUTRAL_MAX` | 20 |
| `cost` | `1 − roundTripSpread / debit` | 10 |

`fwdCushion` is the doctrine's only dollar-honest term: it *is* the loss condition in `kre-a-case-study-in-forward-volatility`. `frontVrp` is the only term that looks outside the chain — it is the house thesis, and it is the one term not collinear with the other three (see 1.4: with a flat forecast, `VRP_f − VRP_b` degenerates to slope). `deltaNeutral` is the trader's hard constraint. `cost` is `illiquid-options` as a number.

`FF` and `slope` are computed and **displayed, not scored** — both are monotone re-expressions of the same two IVs as the cushion. Skew enters as measurement discipline (read IV at 50Δ per expiry; trade one strike) and as one displayed column, `IV(K)/IV(ATM)` per leg — the doctrine's own position is that skew *contaminates* the term-structure read (§2.10), not that it is a source of calendar edge, and §8.13 supplies nothing to calibrate an SPX skew term-structure term with. Greeks are computed and shown; **net vega is not scored** (root-time flat — the one prohibition the dossier states outright, §4.3) and **raw net theta is not scored** (bought with short gamma in exact proportion, §4.10).

**Hard gates, four:** `frontDte >= 15`; `backDte − frontDte >= 15`; liquidity (`bid > 0` both legs, `openInterest >= 100`, spread fraction ≤ 0.10 — the existing `rules.ts:73-75` predicate); `fwdIvGuard === "ok"` (an inverted structure is dropped with a reason, not scored). Plus one kill switch, `if (vix >= 25) return no entries`, and the max-open brake.

**Deliberately OUT of v1:**

1. **Every percentile and rank** — FF band, slope percentile, VIX percentile, IV percentile, VRP rank. 27 days of history makes a 252-sample window ~20 trading days deep; landmine 17 already has the second row ever reporting rank 100. A rank over 20 samples is a random number with a confident label. Ship the levels, accumulate the history — which, per §2 above, needs no migration.
2. **The blended fair-value forecast and any dollar reprice at forecast vol.** One of four inputs is proprietary, two need per-tenor IV history, and the published weights are not self-consistent. RV20 alone is honest; a four-way blend with two invented terms is a fabricated number dressed as a consensus.
3. **The entry gate as a system** — the arm/disarm rungs, the continuous penalty band, the four-tier ladder, the string-matched hysteresis (`entry-gate.ts:80-94, 237-252`). Its own header concedes the three systems move independently, and nobody has evidence the hysteresis ever changed a production outcome.
4. **Kelly and sizing.** §8.17 gives only "they can go to 0" — no p, no b, no distribution. Emit one contract; the human sizes.
5. **Conviction tiers.** The boundary needs either the absolute FF (never fires in contango) or a percentile (see 1). An arbitrary boundary is worse than one number.
6. **Macro-event scoring.** Emit "front spans FOMC/CPI" and "back-only spans X" as row flags, unweighted. The corpus says nothing about SPX macro dates, and the repo already had to soften its own penalty 0.5 → 0.25 because it saturated.
7. **`beVsEm`.** Keep `breakevens.ts` (119 lines, no closed form, returns `[]` rather than fabricating), show the numbers, don't score them. Its target ratio was already walked back 2.0 → 1.5 as unreachable — that is the tell it was measuring the model, not the market.
8. **`gexFit`, `eventAdjustment`, `thetaVega`, `vrp`-as-weighted, `slope`, `context`, `thetaCapturePct`** — five of nine incumbent terms and ~35 of 100 points that current-state shows are cohort-wide constants, near-always satisfied post-gate, or weight-0 display.
9. **Reverse calendars.** Long only until the buy side is proven.
10. **The daily re-entry re-score.** Same `score.ts` called over open calendars instead of candidates — nothing is lost by deferring it, and it needs a second data path.

**One prerequisite, not optional.** `packages/core/src/journal/application/fetchChain.ts:143` (`root: chain.root`) and `:135-138` (local date getters on a UTC-midnight date) must both derive from `parseOccSymbol(quote.occSymbol)`, and `packages/adapters/src/postgres/repos/leg-observations.ts:125` must come off `onConflictDoNothing()` so a backfill can land. I confirmed all three are open at HEAD `6622dec`. The corruption flows through `computeT`'s AM/PM branch into `bsm_iv` and therefore into every IV the engine would read. No engine-side care detects it. Two lines and a migration, before anything above.

**One caveat on `cohort.ts`.** Because `ChainQuoteForPicker` carries no delta, the 50Δ definition depends on the carry the engine resolves. Landmine 13 applies without exception: take r and q from the same expiry row as the IV, or the engine's ATM drifts from the server's.
